Model & assumptions

Two damage channels. Weapon damage is reduced only by bullet resist; spirit damage only by spirit resist. Resists may go negative (amplify).

Weapon damage per bullet is (5.64 + 0.14·boons), boon-driven, then scaled by your Weapon Damage % stat. Headshots deal ×1.65 bullet damage (weapon channel only), averaged by headshot rate, and generate cards faster (5.1% vs 3.4%). Boons grant 1.1 Spirit each on top of the base-spirit slider (+7 with Mercurial) — the totals show under those sliders.

Comparison. The stat purchase sliders layer a hypothetical item on top of your build and show it as a +Stat row (only when above 0). Named items show a +Item row when toggled on; ticking add to baseline folds one into the baseline so every row includes it (and its own row drops out) — use it to ask "given I already own this, what's best next?". Highlighted cells win each column.

Full Auto runs from the start for its duration then reverts (no recast). Card Trick accrues cards per shot (×CDR), thrown on a 0.6s gap with a 0.1s shooting lockout; suit is 1/9 Joker (1/5 at 5AP) else a 2-of-each stack draw. Tesla procs per bullet on a 0.2s cd; Escalating Exposure ramps one amp stack per 0.7s to 12 and amplifies every spirit source.

Modeling choices: the Mercurial card bonus is added after the suit multiplier (so Spades/Joker don't scale it), and its bullet-conversion is a % of the pre-% base bullet damage (boon-driven, unaffected by Weapon Damage %). The table's @3s column is a short-fight reference and DPS @[engagement] uses your set fight length; the chart traces the full sweep.
