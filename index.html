How to use

This tool answers one question: which item should Wraith buy next? Describe your build, pick the items you're considering, and it simulates fights to show which one adds the most damage.

  1. Set your baseline stats. In Base values, enter your boons, headshot rate, and any weapon damage, fire rate, or spirit you already have.
  2. Toggle abilities. Turn Full Auto and Card Trick on or off, and tick any AP upgrades you have leveled. You can also click the card slots to start the fight holding cards.
  3. Choose what items/stats to compare. Each stat slider in Comparison items set above 0, and each item you switch on, adds its own row to the table. If you already own an item, tick its add to baseline box instead: every row then includes it, so the table shows what's best to buy next.
  4. Table explanation. Each row is your build plus that one item or stat, and the highlighted cell in each column is the best result. DPS @3s is a short burst, the next column uses your engagement length, and Total dmg is the damage dealt over that fight. With Time to kill on, the table shows seconds to kill instead, where lower is better.
Modeling details
  • Weapon damage is reduced by bullet resist and spirit damage by spirit resist; shred subtracts from resist, which can go negative and amplify.
  • Bullet damage is (5.64 + 0.14·boons) × (1 + Weapon Damage %); headshots deal ×1.65 on bullet damage only. Boons also give 1.1 spirit each.
  • Numbers are averages over many seeded simulated fights, so the same inputs always give the same results.
  • Abilities and active items are used once per fight at the start (Full Auto first), unless an item lets you set its timing. No recasts.
  • Cards are thrown as soon as they're ready (0.6s apart); a starting hand throws left to right. Suits come from a 2-of-each deck plus a Joker chance.
  • Fire-rate bonuses add together; separate damage reductions multiply.
  • Lucky Shot, Plated Armor, and Armor Piercing Rounds use their average effect rather than rolling each bullet.
  • Past 18m, bullet damage and on-hit effects (Full Auto, Mercurial, Tesla/Capacitor) fall off linearly to 10% at 52m (21.6m and 62.4m with Sharpshooter). Cards and the Capacitor burst don't fall off.
  • Mercurial's card bonus is added after the suit multiplier, and its bullet damage uses base damage before Weapon Damage %.
  • Focus Lens's echo only counts if it lands before the fight ends. Time to kill caps at 60s; a * marks rows that sometimes don't kill in time.
  • Each item's own mechanics are described next to its toggle.
